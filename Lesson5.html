Basics of Financial Management

The Essential Financial Statements

Income Statement

Tracks revenue and expenses over a period. Shows your bottom line: Net Profit or Loss.

Balance Sheet

A snapshot of what you own (Assets) vs. what you owe (Liabilities) and Equity.

Cash Flow Statement

Tracks how much actual cash is moving in and out of the business.
